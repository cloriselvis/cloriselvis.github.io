What gets checked

Expected vs actual

Define the expected business output for each run: records processed, emails sent, invoices updated, rows appended, or leads routed.

Zero-delta alerts

Catch workflows that run successfully but produce no new business result for one or more scheduled runs.

Exception queue

Give skipped records a visible owner, timestamp, reason, and rerun path instead of hiding them in logs.

Good candidates

Revenue workflows

Invoice reminders, quote follow-ups, Stripe status checks, renewal nudges, and CRM lead routing.

Reporting workflows

Weekly Sheets updates, KPI summaries, client reports, dashboards, and data exports that people still manually inspect.

Support handoffs

Gmail, Slack, ticketing, form intake, and AI summaries where missing one record creates visible confusion.
