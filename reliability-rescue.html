What gets fixed

Outcome definition

A clear definition of what "working" means: records seen, records written, skipped reasons, owner, and downstream proof.

Failure map

Likely breakpoints across triggers, queues, wait steps, retries, API writes, callbacks, and notification paths.

Smallest safe repair

The first change to make before a rebuild: audit row, grouped alert, timeout rule, idempotency key, or checker.

Good candidates

Lead workflows

Forms, marketplaces, voice calls, WhatsApp, SMS, email, CRM routing, callbacks, and sales handoffs.

Approval workflows

Slack or email approvals that send a message but sometimes never resume, expire, or update the final system.

Scheduled workflows

Daily or weekly runs that rely on queue timing, API rate limits, Google Sheets, Airtable, or CRM writes.
