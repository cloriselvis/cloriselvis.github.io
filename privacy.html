Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Local processing

LeadLint Studio is a static browser tool. CSV files are parsed and cleaned in your browser. The app does not upload CSV data to a LeadLint server.

Data we do not collect

The local app does not collect contact lists, email addresses inside your CSV, phone numbers, CRM fields, analytics events, or account credentials.

Payment and checkout

If LeadLint Studio is sold through a third-party checkout provider, that provider processes payment, tax, receipt, refund, and fraud-prevention data under its own policies.

Support

If you contact support, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, order ID, and issue description. Do not send confidential customer lists unless a separate service agreement has been established.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the product or sales channel changes. The latest version should be included with the product page or download package.
